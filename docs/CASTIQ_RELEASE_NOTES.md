# Release notes — MVP launch candidate

This candidate keeps the current CastIQ app and changes the launch surface.

Anglers get CastIQ, Search, Ice, and More. More opens About, Pricing, and Tactics. The map remains the CastIQ home screen. Social, regulations, border waters, report sharing, contours, and live intelligence stay in the codebase and are not in the launch menu.

Lake pages lead with the newest public report, then Reports, Surveys & Stocking, Ice, and Water Quality. Repeated copies of the same report are shown once. Search lists lakes by the newest report date, with lakes that have no report at the end.

Pricing shows Standard at $4.99/month or $39.99/year and Premium at $8.99/month or $69.99/year. The buttons do not take payment.

Google sign-in on the Vercel host still depends on the hostname being in Firebase authorized domains, and the auth helper staying on that same host.
