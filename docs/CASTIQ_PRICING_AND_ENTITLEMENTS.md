# Pricing and entitlements

Source: partner pricing reference and `PRICING_SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md`.

| Tier | Interval | Display | Cents if checkout is ever enabled |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard | Monthly | $4.99/month | 499 |
| Standard | Annual | $39.99/year | 3999 |
| Premium | Monthly | $8.99/month | 899 |
| Premium | Annual | $69.99/year | 6999 |

Annual amounts are yearly charges. Billing interval does not change entitlements. Premium includes every Standard entitlement.

`LIVE_CHECKOUT_ENABLED` is false. The plan button says “Coming soon” and tells the visitor CastIQ is not charging. There is no provider, no price id, and no card form.

Premium alerts, extended history windows, advanced filters, lake comparison, and historical trends are not sold as working features. Favorite lakes already exist on the profile for signed-in anglers, which is not the same as a paid saved-lake product.

Unresolved, and not invented here: history length, which filters are “advanced”, any saved-lake cap, and how a new-report alert would be delivered.
